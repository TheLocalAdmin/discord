HypeSquad house script

Your token is your password. This page never asks for it. You paste it into your own browser console, on discord.com only, and nowhere else.

Steps

  1. Open discord.com/app in a desktop browser and log in.
  2. Press F12, go to Network, refresh, filter by /api, and click any request.
  3. Under Request Headers, copy the authorization value.
  4. Open the Console tab and type allow pasting, then Enter.
  5. Paste the script above, replace PASTE_YOUR_TOKEN_HERE (keep the quotes), and press Enter.
  6. ✅ Joined house means done. Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) to see the badge.

Errors: 401 bad or truncated token, 429 rate limited (wait a few minutes), 403 account restricted. If you suspect your token leaked, change your password; that invalidates it. Source: hypesquad.js.
